Fuzzy Boundaries

Fuzzy Boundaries · Interactive image, variable dimensions

I first used Kernel Fuzzy C-Means (KFCM) to segment customer behaviour. What the algorithm actually produces is a membership matrix: every person belongs to every category by a value between 0 and 1. Yet the system's final step is always argmax—it selects the largest value, fixes the person to one label, and discards every other possibility.

Fuzzy Boundaries removes that final step and turns the same algorithm towards images. A pixel is no longer judged as belonging to one colour. It is rendered through its simultaneous memberships across every colour cluster. The fuzziness value m becomes a viewer-controlled lens: as m approaches 1, the world hardens into either/or regions; as m increases, boundaries dissolve and ambiguous territories reappear.

Hover over the image to read a pixel's membership distribution and entropy—the information classification usually erases.
